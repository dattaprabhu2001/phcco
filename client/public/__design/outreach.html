Summer Internship

Summer Internship Programme

The PHCCO Summer Internship Program, held at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, is a six-week immersive research programme designed to train undergraduate and graduate students in interdisciplinary cancer research. The programme combines lectures on cancer systems biology, mathematical and computational modelling, hands-on workshops, and seminars by leading researchers. During the final three weeks, participants undertake mentored research projects, developing predictive models and analysing real-world biological data. By bringing together students from engineering, biology, physics, computer science, and medicine, the programme fosters collaborative, quantitative approaches to understanding cancer and prepares participants for careers in computational and translational oncology.

Workshops

Workshop on Computational Oncology (WoCON)

Workshop on Computational Oncology (WoCON) is an interdisciplinary event focusing on large-scale data analysis in cancer biology, held in collaboration with leading academic institutions across India. The workshop introduces participants to cutting-edge approaches in computational, experimental, and clinical oncology, with emphasis on mathematical modelling, systems biology, artificial intelligence, bioinformatics, and multi-omics data analysis. Through expert lectures, research talks, poster sessions, and interactive discussions, WoCON provides a platform for students and early-career researchers to explore quantitative approaches to understanding cancer progression, foster interdisciplinary collaborations, and gain exposure to emerging computational tools that are transforming cancer diagnosis, prognosis, and therapeutic development.

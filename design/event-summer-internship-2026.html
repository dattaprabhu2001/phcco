Summer Internship Programme 2026

Applications open soon · Summer 2026 · 6 weeks · In person, full time

The PHCCO Summer Internship Program, held at the Indian Institute of Science (IISc), Bengaluru, is a six-week immersive research programme designed to train undergraduate and graduate students in interdisciplinary cancer research.

The programme combines lectures on cancer systems biology, mathematical and computational modelling, hands-on workshops, and seminars by leading researchers. During the final three weeks, participants undertake mentored research projects, developing predictive models and analysing real-world biological data.

By bringing together students from engineering, biology, physics, computer science, and medicine, the programme fosters collaborative, quantitative approaches to understanding cancer and prepares participants for careers in computational and translational oncology.

What to expect

  • Weeks 1–3 — foundations: lectures, hands-on workshops and guest seminars
  • Weeks 4–6 — a mentored research project of your own
  • Work with real biological data and the centre’s researchers
  • Cohorts drawn from engineering, biology, physics, CS and medicine

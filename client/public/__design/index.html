Introduction

About PHCCO

PHCCO — the Param Hansa Centre for Computational Oncology — is India's first dedicated centre for computational oncology, established at IISc Bengaluru in 2023 through a partnership between the Indian Institute of Science and Param Hansa Philanthropies.

More about the centre

Research

Predictive, quantitative models of how cancers develop, evolve and respond to treatment — spanning phenotypic plasticity, tumour evolution, the microenvironment, epigenomics and digital pathology.

Training & Outreach

A six-week immersive summer internship at IISc plus the Workshop on Computational Oncology (WoCON) series, run in collaboration with institutions across India.

Community

A growing community of researchers, clinicians, students and partners — building computational oncology into a genuine force for better cancer outcomes in India.
Why we exist

Mission

Param Hansa Philanthropies was founded on a conviction: that the convergence of computation and biology holds the key to addressing humanity's hardest health challenges — and that India is uniquely positioned to lead that effort. PHCCO is PHP's expression of that conviction. It is not a grant. It is a long-term commitment to building an institution that changes what cancer research looks like in India.

The convergence of AI, multi-omics, digital pathology and clinical data is creating a genuine window to move computational oncology from insight to clinical impact — and India is positioned to lead that transition, not follow it.

PHP's partnership with IISc and its investment in PHCCO is a bet on that potential — and on the scientists, students, and collaborators who are making it real.
